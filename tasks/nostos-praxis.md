# Nostos: a practical activity per chapter (proposal, 7 November 2026)

Maria's idea: alongside the lessons and the reflections, something to **do** in each chapter, such as a special meditation, a challenge (not doing something, or doing something every day), or taking and uploading a photo that the AI looks at. Athena's own line for it: "Awakening that does not become life is incomplete awakening." Working name: **Praxis**, a third kind of stone on the trail.

## Six kinds of practical activity

| Kind | What the learner does | What the AI does | Without AI |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Meditation** | A guided sit, 5 to 20 minutes: one step at a time on screen, a soft bell between steps, a timer; one line afterwards: "what did you notice?" | Reads the line and answers in one or two sentences in Athena's terms (optional) | Works fully |
| **Challenge** | 3 to 7 days: do something daily, or stop doing something. A tick and one line each day, a streak, and a closing note on the last day | At the end, reads the week's lines and writes a short reading of what changed and where it caught | The ticks and the streak work; no reading |
| **Photo** | Take or upload a photo that answers a prompt (a shadow and what casts it, beauty on your way, something "the same and never the same") | Looks at it (Claude can see images) and answers through Athena's idea, gently checking that it fits the prompt; never grades harshly | A self-check: "does your photo show…?" |
| **Mission** | Do one thing out in the world today (ask someone a Socratic question, one act for your street), then report in two lines | Answers the report | The report is kept |
| **Make** | Write or draw something: your own hymn, a Platonic dialogue, a letter to your future self | Reads it and answers | It is kept |
| **Ritual** | A dated practice for a real day: the next solstice or equinox, the next festival | Fits Athena's steps to the date and your note | A calendar file (.ics) to add the day to your calendar |

**For all of them:**
- **Grounding:** everything rests on Athena's 173 practices (the number in brackets below is the practice in `src/data/nostos/practices.json`).
- **Privacy:** photos and notes stay in the browser and go only to Anthropic, with the visitor's own key, as for the tutor.
- **On the path:**
  - a third kind of stone, perhaps an oil lamp, an olive leaf or a sandal;
  - it never blocks the path;
  - a challenge in progress shows its days filling in on the stone.
- **Reminders:** a static site cannot send reminders by itself. A challenge can offer a calendar file with a reminder each day. Email or push reminders would need accounts later.
- **Overlaps:** this is close to the existing Askesis activity (one practice a day) and the Practical Guide's habits. Praxis would give them a place on the path and a beginning and an end.

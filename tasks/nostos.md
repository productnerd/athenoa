# Nostos: a study space for Athena's teaching

Status: data ready on branch `nostos` (all 26 talks, 10 writings, bookshelf of 18 works with 65 passages). Nothing built in the site yet. Read this file, `DESIGN.md` and `tasks/lessons.md` before starting.

## What it is

Athena has no book. Nostos (νόστος, the homecoming: her reading of the Odyssey as the soul's journey home) turns her long-form talks and writings into a place where a visitor can study her teaching by doing, not by reading. It was first built as a prototype in a Claude chat (`reference/nostos-prototype/`). This task is to bring it into the Athenoa site, keeping the concept, the content and every feature, but following DESIGN.md completely for look, type, motion and tone.

The prototype's own look (a night-sky palette, seven theme colours, an XP counter) does NOT carry over. Port behaviour and content, not styling.

## What Maria asked for (keep all of it)

1. A collection of Athena's work, built from all her long-form talks (her own channel and podcasts or interviews on other channels) and her writings.
2. Interactive and playful study, "lots of activities, not lots of reading", engaging and satisfying.
3. Mental links between her ideas and the learner's own knowledge and interests, to make them memorable.
4. A connected concept map: each node an idea (enlightenment, critical thinking, Aristotle and so on), joined to the ideas it is thematically connected to, with node size proportional to how often the idea comes up in her teaching.
5. A tab of her favourite readings as a bookshelf, in the spirit of press.stripe.com: a 3D book that comes off the shelf and opens to show what the work is and how her ideas link to it.
6. Her writings (papers, articles, essays) alongside the talks.

## The content (src/data/nostos)

All of it is NEW WORDING, generated by Claude from YouTube auto-captions (17 talks) and Whisper transcripts made on Maria's Mac (9 talks), plus 7 readable writings. It is in English, with Greek terms in Greek script. It is for Athena to review before launch, in the same spirit as the comment at the top of `src/data/lemmas.ts`. Treat it as data, not copy to rewrite, and add a short header comment saying so wherever it is imported.

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `meta.json` | count of transcribed talks; the seven theme groups (`path`, `soul`, `ethics`, `cosmos`, `ways`, `thinkers`, `gods`) with labels |
| `concepts.json` | 152 ideas: `id`, `label`, `greek`, `translit`, `kind`, `group`, `take` (what it means in her teaching), `more` (other angles), `weight` 1 to 5, `freq` (mentions across transcripts; `est: true` means estimated from weight because the idea was not in the counted vocabulary), `talks` (`{id, t, note}`: where she says it, with timestamp), `links` (`{to, why}`: connections she makes herself) |
| `edges.json` | 1,050 links: `a`, `b`, `her` (she links them herself), `co` and `npmi` (how often she discusses them within the same ~5 minutes, and how strongly), `why` |
| `talks.json` | 26 talks: `id` (YouTube id), `title`, `en` (English title), `channel`, `dur`, `lang`, `status` |
| `writings.json` | 10 writings: `id` (`w_...`), `title`, `title_en`, `venue`, `year`, `lang`, `kind`, `url`, `readable`, `summary`, `key_ideas`, `terms`, `concepts` |
| `quiz.json` | 383 multiple-choice items: `q`, `a`, `wrong[3]`, `talk`, `t`, `explain` |
| `truefalse.json` | 208 items: `s`, `true`, `explain`, `talk` |
| `sequences.json` | 78 ordered sets of steps she teaches (cave ascent, Diotima's ladder, Apollo's five names, ...): `title`, `steps[]`, `note`, `talk`, `t` |
| `myths.json` | 119 stories told beat by beat with her reading of each beat: `title`, `beats[{beat, reading}]`, `talk` |
| `etymologies.json` | 174 word roots: `word`, `translit`, `parts[{part, meaning}]`, `reading`, `status` (`hers` = her own reading, `standard`, `disputed`), `talk` |
| `distinctions.json` | 237 pairs she draws (ζῆν / εὖ ζῆν, ...): `a`, `a_gloss`, `b`, `b_gloss`, `point`, `talk` |
| `practices.json` | 155 things to do: `title`, `steps[]`, `why`, `talk` |
| `images.json`, `questions.json`, `lines.json` | her analogies, the questions she puts to listeners, and short lines (under 15 words, translated) |
| `library.json` | the bookshelf (18 works, 65 passages): `works[{id, title, author, greek_title, why_she_reads_it, talks, weight, excerpts[{ref, label, greek, translation, translator, source_url, her_reading, talk, t}], next_step}]`, `reading_path[]`. Excerpts are original Greek plus public-domain translations published before 1929 (Jowett, Taylor, Butler, Burnet and so on), copied from the fetched source pages. |

Caveats that matter for presentation:
- `uncertain: true` marks anything reconstructed from garbled captions. Show it quietly (a small mark), never hide the item.
- Etymologies marked `hers` are her own readings, some of which scholars dispute. Say so in the UI, kindly ("her reading").
- A few contested empirical claims (about the pandemic, about physics) are kept only as "she says", never as quiz facts. Keep it that way.
- In interviews the host's ideas were kept out of her teaching. Talk `status` and the notes behind it say which talks are interviews.
- Frequencies count Greek and English stems with regexes over full transcripts; they are loose (everyday uses get counted too). Treat size as a fair relative signal, not a precise statistic. `self` was damped for this reason.

Personal "bridges" (links from her ideas to Maria's own life, used by the prototype's Threads activity) are deliberately NOT in the repo, because it is public. See Threads below.

## Mapping the prototype onto the Athenoa design system

Port every behaviour; restyle every surface. Use the kit in DESIGN.md (`.panel`, `.box`, `.btn`, `.hand`, `.hl`, `<Illustration>`, `<Media>`, lemma notes, motion attributes). Nothing in a new hue.

**Star map (front door).**
- Sky: a large `.panel panel--big on-blue-deep` with a rough edge, like the night version of the hero. Background stars from the existing star drawings (`hero-b-star`, `portal-b-stars-*`) as a still layer. No looping star animation.
- Stars: the seven theme groups cannot have seven colours. Encode the theme by star shape (for example round, four-point, eight-point, ringed, diamond, cross, asterisk) and use the three blues plus crème for tone. For example, crème for the brightest ideas, `--blue-light` for the rest, and outlines for themes that need separating. Keep a legend that toggles themes on and off.
- Size: radius from `freq` with a square-root scale (prototype: 5 to 36 px).
- Layout: run d3-force at build time (Node, in the Astro page's frontmatter or a script) and ship the final x and y in the page. The client must not run a simulation (Maria's machine is slow, see lessons). The client only pans, zooms and highlights. Prototype layout settings: use each star's three strongest links (`score` = her link 0.6 + npmi × 2.2 + log1p(co) × 0.18 + (weight a + weight b) / 18) for the layout, anchor each theme on an ellipse (540 by 300) so themes form regions, set charge at −120 − r × 14 and collide at r + 10.
- Lines: show the strongest three per star by default, with toggles for "Her links", "Said together" and "All". A selected star shows all its own links.
- Labels: show the 34 biggest by default, and more as you zoom. Keep text crème on deep blue at a constant screen size.
- Selecting a star opens a paper note (`.box`, paper texture) beside the map, or as a sheet on phones. It holds the Greek word large in EB Garamond, the transliteration, the label, the "mentioned N times" line, her take, other angles, roots (from etymologies), linked ideas with her reason (clickable), and "where she says it" with timestamped YouTube links. It also offers "I know this one", "Quiz me on it" and (if enabled) "Spar on it". The site's lemma-note style is a natural model for the Greek head of the note.
- "Find the link" game: one star pulses (via the opacity or transform of an HTML overlay, never an SVG animation), a clue shows one of her `why` sentences, and the visitor taps the other star. Three tries.
- Search finds a star by label, Greek or transliteration, accent-insensitively.

**Play (activities).** Twelve activities, all drawn from the JSON. See `startActivity()` and the `act*` functions in the prototype:
- Logos cards: Leitner spaced repetition over concepts that have Greek, with boxes 0 to 5 at intervals of 0, 1, 3, 7, 16 and 35 days.
- Match race: six Greek words against six meanings, timed.
- Oracle: ten multiple-choice questions, each with an explanation and the talk to check.
- Lightning: true or false for 60 seconds, with streaks.
- Ladders: put a sequence in order. Order is the content here, so show the rungs as a drawn ladder rather than numbered markers.
- Myth decoder: a story beat by beat; choose her reading of each.
- Root forge: pick a word's roots from tiles that include decoys, then see her reading, marked "her reading", "standard" or "disputed".
- The fork: the Pythagorean Υ. Which side of her distinction does a gloss belong to?
- Threads: see below.
- Socratic sparring and Teach it back: see "Claude-powered activities".
- Askesis: one practice per day, plus one of her questions to carry.

Styling: no identical card grid (DESIGN "Avoid"). Vary the box shapes (`--r-pebble-*`, `--r-soft*`, `--r-leaf`) and sizes, and use symbol drawings (`lyre`, `torch`, `olive-branch`, `cave-sun`, `sun-rays`, `dove-scroll`, `bow-lyre` and so on) instead of the prototype's Greek-letter glyphs. Feedback is quiet and adult: no confetti, no bouncing, no owls, nothing that reads as Duolingo. Right and wrong are shown by ink and a small mark, not green and red (there are no other hues).

**Progress.** Keep Diotima's ladder as the progress idea: seven rungs from "one beautiful body" to "Beauty itself", reached by experience points from activities. It suits the brand and the teaching. Keep the counter inside Nostos only (no pill in the site header). Also keep "stars you know" and the memory boxes. Store everything in `localStorage` under one key (prototype shape: `{xp, days, srs, known, best, done}`). Supabase accounts and sync can come later if Maria wants them.

**Bookshelf.** Book spines on a shelf. Keep the Stripe Press idea: hover eases a book out, and a click brings it forward, turns it to its cover, then opens the cover on its hinge to show the inside page (prototype classes `book3d`, `cover`, `page` and their CSS transitions). In Athenoa terms:
- Spines are blue cut-outs in the three shades plus crème ones, with the rough-edge filter on a decorative layer only. The cover carries a symbol drawing.
- Pages are crème paper with grain.
- The inside shows "why she reads it", the passage in public-domain translation with the original Greek behind a toggle, "her reading" (an annotation, so the voice face `.hand` fits here, at `.hand--m` or larger), links into the star map, and the talks where she reads it.
- The reading path shows as a quiet row under the shelf.
- On phones the book opens to a single page.
- The 3D turn uses transform only; respect reduced motion (then simply show the open page).

**Sources.** Talks and writings as two lists, each linking to its concepts on the map and out to YouTube or the original page.

**Type and copy.** Titles in the title face, reading text in Tekst, Greek in EB Garamond (check accented Greek renders: lessons, 2026-10-10). The voice face is only for her own short lines (`lines.json`) and annotations. Never use it for body text or controls. Interface labels for Nostos go in a new `src/data/nostos/ui.ts` (en and el), marked as new wording.

**Motion and performance.** Use the site's attribute system (`data-reveal`, `data-reveal-group`, `data-draw`) for entrances. Nothing loops inside an SVG. Run `node scripts/perf.mjs` on the map page at rest. The map's SVG must be cheap: precomputed positions, no filters on the moving layer.

## Threads (personal links) in a public site

The prototype tied her ideas to Maria's own world (freediving, her values map, Cypriot dialect, game design). For visitors, make Threads a "make your own link" activity instead: after a concept, ask "what in your life does this remind you of?", keep the visitor's answer in localStorage, and show it on that star's note next time, so the mnemonic hook is theirs. Optionally offer a few generic prompts (craft, sport, nature, family). Maria's own hooks can be added later as a private option if she wants them.

## Claude-powered activities

Socratic sparring (Claude questions the learner for five rounds on one idea) and Teach it back (the learner explains an idea; Claude scores it 1 to 5 against her teaching and lists what was got, missed or misread) ran on claude.ai's built-in sampling. The prompts are in the prototype's `actSpar` and `actTeach`, built from the concept's `take`, `more` and `links`. On the public site they need a small server function (Cloudflare Worker or similar, since the site is static) calling the Claude API with Maria's key, with rate limiting and short replies. Decide with Maria; until then build them behind a flag and hide them.
